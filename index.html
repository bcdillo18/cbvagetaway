Why Stay in Colonial Beach, Virginia?

Colonial Beach is one of Virginia's best waterfront vacation destinations, known for its sandy beaches, boating, fishing, golf cart-friendly streets, and laid-back coastal charm. Located within driving distance of Washington, D.C. and Richmond, it offers the perfect weekend or extended getaway.
